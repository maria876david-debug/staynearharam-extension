# Welcome to StayNearHaram: Hajj and Umrah Guides You Can Trust

StayNearHaram is a new guide site for pilgrims planning Hajj or Umrah. It covers the three cities most trips are built around — Makkah, Madinah and Taif — with a deep guide for every major ziyarat, honest pages on where to stay near the Haram, what food costs, and what a full trip is likely to come to before you book anything. Every guide is written the same way: the real process, in plain order, with prices only where a source supports them, so you can plan a calm trip instead of guessing your way through it. The Makkah section is already complete, and Madinah is being built now.
