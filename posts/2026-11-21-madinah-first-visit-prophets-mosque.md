# Madinah on a First Visit: What to See Around the Prophet's Mosque

Most first-time visitors plan every day in Makkah and leave Madinah as an afterthought - and that is a mistake. Madinah rewards a slower pace. Start at the Prophet's Mosque itself: arrive early for Fajr, when the courtyards are calm, and spend time in the Rawdah, the blessed area between the Prophet's house and his pulpit, which fills quickly so plan the visit and any permit in advance. Around the mosque, Quba Mosque - the first mosque built in Islam - is a short ride away and worth a morning. Uhud, where the mountain and the martyrs' graveyard sit, is best visited with a guide who can tell the story of the place properly. Practical notes: hotels near the mosque are worth the price for the walk at prayer time, especially with elderly travellers, and the evenings around the mosque are cooler and less crowded than the midday heat.
