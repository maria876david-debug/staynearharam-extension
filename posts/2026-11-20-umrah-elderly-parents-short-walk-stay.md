# Umrah with Elderly Parents: Choosing a Stay That Keeps the Walk Short and Easy

Taking elderly parents for Umrah is one of the best gifts a family can give - and the hotel choice decides whether the trip is gentle or exhausting. Distance on a map is not the measure; the walk is. A hotel 400 metres away up a slope can be harder than one 700 metres away on flat ground with a shuttle. Ask three questions before booking: is the route to the Haram flat, does the hotel run a shuttle to the mosque, and how far is the drop-off point from the doors? Rooms matter too - a bathroom with grab support, a lift that does not take ten minutes at prayer time, and breakfast included so the first meal needs no outing. Plan the days around their strength: one Umrah performed slowly is worth more than a schedule that leaves them in pain. Their duas at the Kaaba are the point of the trip; everything else is logistics in service of that.
