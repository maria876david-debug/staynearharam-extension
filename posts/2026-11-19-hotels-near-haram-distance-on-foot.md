# Hotels Near the Haram: What the Distance Really Means on Foot

A hotel listed as "near the Haram" can mean a two-minute walk or a fifteen-minute one - and in Makkah and Madinah, that difference matters more than the price. The walk is not flat: there are slopes, crowds, prayer-time road closures, and in summer, heat that turns a short walk into a hard one, especially for older travellers and children. Before booking, check the real walking route on a map, not the straight-line distance, and ask whether the hotel runs a shuttle and how often. A hotel a little further away with a reliable shuttle can be easier than a closer one with no shade on the walk. Book the distance your feet can manage five times a day, every day of the trip - not the distance that looks good on the listing.
