# StayNearHaram — Umrah Trip Checklist

Plan your Hajj or Umrah trip with a quick essentials checklist and StayNearHaram guides.

StayNearHaram helps you plan a Hajj or Umrah trip with honest guides for Makkah, Madinah and Taif — ziyarat guides, where to stay, what things cost, and how to get around. This extension gives you a quick trip-essentials checklist right from your browser, plus one-click access to StayNearHaram's Makkah and Madinah guides and honest trip costs. Anything you tick stays on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/staynearharam-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://staynearharam.com

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
